## Our project

**Who Owns The Sky Above You**

Pick a city, see every satellite above it right now, and ask any of them who they belong to.

- Live orbital data: CelesTrak + satellite.js
- Rendering: three.js (OSINT console aesthetic)
- Ownership + purpose + sources: **Cala** entity graph
- AI answers from verified data only; refuses when no source exists

## Main track: Cala — "Trip to Another Hackathon"

Pick a topic you're curious about, use Cala to dig into it, and find the most compelling way to show what you found. Infographic, interactive piece, game, film, poem — whatever the story needs.

> Good data deserves to be seen, not just queried.

### Judging criteria

1. **Creativity & originality** — an angle or format they haven't seen before
2. **Storytelling & clarity** — does the piece actually communicate something
3. **Execution & craft** — polished, feels finished rather than rushed
4. **Use of Cala** — how deeply Cala's data shaped the final piece
